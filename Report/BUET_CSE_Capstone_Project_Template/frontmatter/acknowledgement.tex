\chapter*{ACKNOWLEDGEMENT}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Acknowledgement}

We thank our supervisor, Dr.\ A.\ B.\ M.\ Alim Al Islam, Professor in the
Department of Computer Science and Engineering at BUET. Beyond his supervision
of the work itself, he introduced us to a range of real applications and
websites to test against, and in doing so widened the scope of the project well
past what we had first proposed. A system that had been built for one
application became one that had to work for many, and that change of perimeter
did more to shape the result than any decision we took on our own.

We thank Samsung Research and Development Institute Bangladesh for posing the
problem and for the guidance offered along the way. The technical requirements
document they supplied gave the project a real commission with real
constraints rather than an exercise of our own devising, and their review of an
early iteration identified the dependence on hard-coded, application-specific
values that we would otherwise have carried much further than we did.

Finally, we thank each other. This was a five-person project in which every
member owned a component and was answerable for it, and the parts only ever fit
together because people were willing to have their work reviewed, corrected and
occasionally switched off in favour of a better argument.
