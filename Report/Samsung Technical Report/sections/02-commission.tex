\section{Commission and Scope}
\label{sec:commission}

\subsection{What was asked for}

ETA-TR-2026-001 sets out to evolve the existing agent from a static-knowledge
test generator into a system that accumulates experiential knowledge,
prioritises defect-prone areas, shortens re-execution through learned
navigation, and adapts across device profiles, platforms and applications. Its
guiding principle is stated in the document as: every execution cycle must make
the agent smarter than the last.

The document specifies eight requirement families, ETA-REQ-301 to ETA-REQ-308,
two non-functional requirements, and a six-phase implementation ordering with a
total estimated effort of 17 to 23 weeks. \Cref{sec:fulfilment} reports each
against the evidence.

\subsection{What the team added, and why}

Contact with real applications produced eleven further requirements that the
commissioning document does not contain. They are listed here because several
of them changed the architecture, and because three in particular are the
reason the results in \Cref{sec:results} can be read at all.

\subsection{Constraints the work operated under}

Four constraints shaped what could be built and what could be measured, and
they are stated here so that the results are read against them.

\begin{description}[leftmargin=2.6em,style=nextline,itemsep=3pt,topsep=4pt]
  \item[No accelerator hardware.] No GPU allocation was available, so hosted
    inference through a commercial aggregator was the only route to a capable
    model. Every cost in \Cref{sec:economics} is therefore metered API spend.
  \item[No defect ground truth.] Neither a defect export nor a seeded-defect
    build was available, which is what prevents precision and recall being
    reported.
  \item[A production target with live users.] The principal Android target is
    in continuous use, so a class of operations had to be placed out of scope
    and enforced rather than exercised.
  \item[No stable identifiers on that target.] Its cross-platform toolkit
    exposes almost no resource identifiers through the accessibility tree and
    reports one activity for every screen, so screens must be told apart
    structurally. This constraint drove more engineering than any other single
    fact about the targets.
\end{description}
