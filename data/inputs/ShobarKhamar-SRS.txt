Software Requirements Specification — ShobarKhamar Android Application
Package: com.tirzokpvt.shobarkhamar   Framework: Flutter   Build under test: 2.1.3

ShobarKhamar is a livestock and agricultural marketplace connecting farmers, buyers,
cattle sellers, feed sellers and supporting services. Users discover cattle, feed,
medicine and equipment; sellers publish listings and manage incoming order requests;
buyers favourite products, request orders, review sellers and chat with them. The app
also provides livestock disease information with AI-assisted health checking, and uses
device location to rank nearby farms and products.

SCOPE NOTE FOR THIS TEST CAMPAIGN
The device is signed in with a pre-provisioned account before the campaign starts.
Sections marked PRECONDITION-SATISFIED below describe behaviour that is already in
place and MUST NOT be re-tested: completing them requires an SMS one-time password
delivered to a phone we do not control, a national identity document, platform admin
approval, or a phone number that is consumed permanently on first use. They are listed
so their outcomes can be relied upon, not so they can be exercised. Every other section
is in scope.

1. AUTHENTICATION AND ACCOUNT STATE  [PRECONDITION-SATISFIED — do not test]
FR-AUTH-01 An account exists and is registered. Registration is not re-tested.
FR-AUTH-02 The account is signed in with a phone number and password before testing begins.
FR-AUTH-03 The account's role is already selected and, for seller roles, already approved.
FR-AUTH-04 One-time password verification has already been completed for this account.
FR-AUTH-05 Password recovery and password change are not exercised; either would lock
           the campaign out of the account it depends on.
FR-AUTH-06 Account deletion is not exercised; it would destroy the account under test.
FR-AUTH-07 The signed-in session persists across app restarts, so tests may assume an
           authenticated session at all times.

2. NAVIGATION AND DISCOVERY
FR-NAV-01 A splash screen is shown before the application becomes interactive.
FR-NAV-02 A primary navigation bar exposes Home, Blog, Order, Search and Menu.
FR-NAV-03 The home screen presents discovery entry points: Browse Cattle, Sell Cow,
          Feed, Medicine, Equipment and Farmer Market.
FR-NAV-04 A search entry point accepts free text for livestock, farms and feed.
FR-NAV-05 Product, farm and store detail views open from any discovery card or list row.
FR-NAV-06 Lists that can return more results support pagination or lazy loading.
FR-NAV-07 Remote data retrieval presents readable loading, empty and failure states.
FR-NAV-08 A location selector in the header sets the active region for discovery.

3. PRODUCT AND MARKETPLACE BROWSING
FR-PROD-01 Cattle listings are browsable with their attributes and imagery.
FR-PROD-02 Feed products are browsable by type and brand.
FR-PROD-03 Medicine and veterinary items are browsable where the role is enabled.
FR-PROD-04 Equipment items are browsable where the role is enabled.
FR-PROD-05 Product detail shows price, description, attributes and seller identity.
FR-PROD-06 Product and seller content can be shared through the device share sheet.
FR-PROD-07 Filters narrow results by category, type, breed, price and location.
FR-PROD-08 A product that is unavailable or withdrawn is presented as such rather than
           opening a broken detail view.

4. FAVOURITES
FR-FAV-01 A signed-in user adds a marketplace product to favourites.
FR-FAV-02 A signed-in user removes a product from favourites.
FR-FAV-03 The favourites list shows exactly the products currently favourited.
FR-FAV-04 Adding to favourites produces a visible acknowledgement.
FR-FAV-05 Favourite state survives leaving and re-entering the product detail view.

5. ORDER REQUESTS
FR-ORD-01 A buyer initiates an order request for an eligible product.
FR-ORD-02 The order form captures the information the backend requires.
FR-ORD-03 Submitting produces an explicit success or failure outcome.
FR-ORD-04 The buyer's order list shows their submitted order requests.
FR-ORD-05 Order detail shows status, product and seller information.
FR-ORD-06 A seller views incoming order requests for their own listings.
FR-ORD-07 A seller opens order detail and updates status to a supported value.
FR-ORD-08 The buyer sees the updated status after the seller changes it.
FR-ORD-09 Feed sellers have equivalent order list, detail and status workflows.
FR-ORD-09a Medicine sellers have equivalent order list, detail and status workflows.
FR-ORD-09b Machinery/equipment sellers have equivalent order list, detail and status workflows.
FR-ORD-10 An empty order list, or a filter matching nothing, shows a clear empty state.
FR-ORD-11 Failures in order retrieval, creation or status update surface an error.
FR-ORD-12 Submitting the same order twice does not silently create duplicates.

6. FARM AND ANIMAL MANAGEMENT  (seller roles)
FR-FARM-01 A seller registers farm information including name and farm type.
FR-FARM-02 Farm registration captures animal type or category where required.
FR-FARM-03 Farm location is set from device location or a map picker.
FR-FARM-04 Mandatory farm fields are validated, and missing ones are named specifically.
FR-FARM-05 A farm owner updates farm information after registration.
FR-FARM-06 Successful and failed farm updates both produce meaningful feedback.
FR-ANM-01 A seller adds an animal record through a multi-step flow.
FR-ANM-02 The flow supports cattle type, sub-type and breed selection.
FR-ANM-03 Additional animal attributes are captured and shown on the listing.
FR-ANM-04 Abandoning the multi-step flow midway does not create a partial record.

7. SELLER STORE AND PRODUCT PUBLISHING  (cattle / feed / medicine / machinery sellers)
FR-SELL-01 A seller publishes a cattle listing with price, attributes and images.
FR-SELL-02 A seller edits an existing listing.
FR-SELL-03 A seller changes a listing's availability status.
FR-SELL-04 A feed seller registers a feed store and publishes feed products.
FR-SELL-05 Listing input is validated before submission, naming invalid fields.
FR-SELL-06 A published listing appears in the buyer-facing marketplace.
FR-SELL-07 A feed seller edits feed product details and availability.
FR-SELL-08 A medicine seller registers a store and publishes veterinary medicine items.
FR-SELL-09 A medicine seller edits a medicine item and changes its availability.
FR-SELL-10 A machinery/equipment seller registers a store and publishes equipment items.
FR-SELL-11 A machinery/equipment seller edits an equipment item and changes its availability.
FR-SELL-12 Each seller role sees only its own listings and its own incoming orders.
FR-SELL-13 A seller role cannot reach another role's publishing or order screens.
FR-SELL-14 Listing images are attachable from the device gallery or camera where the
           product type supports imagery.

8. REVIEWS AND SELLER TRUST
FR-REV-01 Seller reviews are viewable from a seller profile.
FR-REV-02 A seller rating summary is shown where ratings exist.
FR-REV-03 A buyer submits a review for a seller after an eligible interaction.
FR-REV-04 Review input is validated before submission.
FR-REV-05 Successful review submission is acknowledged and the review appears.
FR-REV-06 Review lists paginate where more reviews exist.
FR-REV-07 A seller with no reviews shows an empty state, not an error.

9. CHAT
FR-CHT-01 A chat inbox lists existing conversations.
FR-CHT-02 A conversation shows message history in order.
FR-CHT-03 A user sends a message to a seller or farmer.
FR-CHT-04 A sent message appears in the conversation immediately.
FR-CHT-05 An empty inbox shows an empty state rather than a failure.

10. NOTIFICATIONS
FR-NOT-01 A notification list shows received notifications.
FR-NOT-02 An unread count is displayed and reflects unread notifications.
FR-NOT-03 Notifications are marked read, and the unread count updates.
FR-NOT-04 An empty notification list shows an empty state.
FR-NOT-05 Supported notification deep links open the referenced content.

11. LOCATION
FR-LOC-01 Location permission is requested when a feature needs device location.
FR-LOC-02 Nearby filtering ranks farms and products by proximity when permitted.
FR-LOC-03 The current device location can be selected or confirmed on a map.
FR-LOC-04 Denying location permission leaves non-location functionality usable.
FR-LOC-05 Concurrent duplicate location requests are prevented.
FR-LOC-06 Changing the active region updates discovery results accordingly.

MEDIA NOTE
Requirements FR-SELL-01, FR-SELL-14 and FR-DIS-03 require attaching an image. Test
images are pre-seeded in the device gallery, so these flows are IN SCOPE: pick an
existing image rather than expecting the camera to produce one.

12. DISEASE INFORMATION AND HEALTH CHECK
FR-DIS-01 A list of common livestock diseases is browsable.
FR-DIS-02 Disease detail presents symptoms, prevention and remedy information.
FR-DIS-03 An AI-assisted health check accepts an image for disease inference.
FR-DIS-04 An inference result is presented with its limitations made clear.
FR-DIS-05 A failed or unavailable inference produces an error, not a blank result.

13. PROFILE AND SETTINGS
FR-PROF-01 The signed-in user's profile is displayed.
FR-PROF-02 Supported profile fields are editable and persist after saving.
FR-PROF-03 A seller profile is viewable by buyers and by the seller themselves.
FR-PROF-04 A profile can be shared via a shareable link.
FR-SET-01 Settings expose the options supported for the signed-in role.
FR-SET-02 Signing out ends the session and returns to an unauthenticated state.
           (Do not exercise: signing out requires OTP to sign back in.)

14. NON-FUNCTIONAL
NFR-01 Primary screens become interactive without unreasonable delay on a stable network.
NFR-02 Long lists paginate rather than loading unbounded result sets.
NFR-03 Network, image and inference work does not block the interface.
NFR-04 Backend failures are reported to the user rather than failing silently.
NFR-05 The interface remains usable when the device is offline or the network is slow.
NFR-06 Destructive or irreversible actions require explicit confirmation.
